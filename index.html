Mga Madalas Itanong

Do I need coding or design skills?
No. You fill in simple fields about your own or your client's business, and Pahina Builder writes the copy and designs the page for you.
Is there a monthly fee?
No. You pay once and keep lifetime access. The app runs on your own AI key, which has a free tier, so there is no monthly AI bill.
How many funnels can I build?
As many as you need. The app has no limit. The only limit is the daily usage of your own AI key.
Can I build funnels for clients and charge them?
Yes. Your license covers unlimited pages for unlimited clients. You may not resell the app itself unless you get the White Label license.
I'm not techy. Is setup hard?
You get a step-by-step Master Guide with pictures, from getting your AI key to publishing your first page.
How do I get access after paying?
Automatically. Right after payment you get an email with your access link and license key. One click unlocks the app on your device.
Why not just use ChatGPT?
A chatbot gives you text. Pahina Builder gives you a finished, designed page with checkout, chatbot, booking and lead capture already working, ready to publish.
Where do I put my pages online?
Download the page and publish it on hosting like Vercel, Netlify or GitHub Pages, or on your client's own website.
What is the White Label license?
For $198 one-time, you can put your own name, logo and colors on the app and sell it as your own product. It also includes every Suite tool for your own business.
What if it doesn't work for me?
If you can't build a sales funnel within one day of buying, message us and we'll give you a full refund.
