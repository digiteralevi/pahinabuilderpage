Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Pahina Builder, hosting, and payments.

Do I need any programming or design skills to use this?
None at all. If you know how to fill out a 3-minute form, you can create a complete, ready-to-sell landing page. The AI takes care of layout architecture, color harmony, typography hierarchy, persuasive copy, and code export automatically.
What is the Gemini API key and does it cost money?
You only need a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. It takes less than 60 seconds to create, costs zero money, and the application includes a clear step-by-step walkthrough showing you how to paste it into your settings.
How do my customers pay me?
Pahina Builder connects directly to the payment channels you already use: GCash, Maya, PayMongo, PayPal, Stripe, Wise, or Cash On Delivery. When customers check out on your page, funds go directly to your accounts without platform cuts.
Are there really no monthly subscription charges?
Yes, this is a genuine one-time license. You pay once for lifetime access to the software. You will never see a recurring monthly or yearly subscription bill from us for the Pahina Builder platform.
Where do my published sales pages live?
Your pages are 100% yours. You can download the complete clean code file and upload it anywhere you like, including free hosting services like Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages. Connect your own custom domain at no extra charge.
Can I build sales pages on my smartphone?
Absolutely. Pahina Builder is fully web-based and responsive. You can draft an offer, generate the copy, preview the layout, and deploy directly from your mobile browser while on the move.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a monthly fee?
No. It's a one-time payment for lifetime access to Pahina Builder.
What do I need to use it?
A browser and a free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. The app shows you how to get one in about a minute.
How do I get access after paying?
You get an email right away with your license key and an auto-unlock link. Click it on each device you use.
Do I need coding or design skills?
No. You answer simple questions, pick a template and click Generate. You can still edit any text or image after.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. It works in the browser on phone, tablet and laptop.
Where do I publish my page?
Download it and upload to Vercel, Netlify or GitHub Pages. They have free plans, and the app includes a step-by-step guide.
What payment methods can my page accept?
GCash, Maya, PayPal, Stripe, any payment link, or cash on delivery.
Can my page be in English or Tagalog?
Yes. Choose English, Tagalog/Taglish or other languages before you generate.
Is there a chatbot on my page?
Yes. The Smart FAQ Chatbot answers your visitors' common questions and sends the rest to your Messenger or WhatsApp.
